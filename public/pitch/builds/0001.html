How to test with two windows

Two windows: keep this one as Host, open the same link in a second window and press Investor view there. One window: just flip between Host (you) and Investor view in the top bar; the game keeps running.
The call: the investor sees a pre-join screen first, then appears as a small tile next to you. The People tab above your chat lists everyone, with Audience mode, Mute all and Let speak for raised hands.
New commands: the game invents commands as people type. Common actions (sit down, spin, cry, fly and about thirty more) are made instantly. AI harness: it switches on with Chat controls; anything the investor types that is not already a command is sent to Claude, which invents an animation for it. Chat shows "new command created" and the command then works for everyone. It uses your Claude usage and asks your permission the first time.
Room 1: talk through the slides, then switch on Chat controls. The investor types to search the room for the key ("check the cupboard"), then "open the door". Give hint and Skip puzzle are there if they get stuck.
Keys: click the game first. As host, A and D (or the arrow keys) walk your presenter along the stage and space jumps. As a joined investor, WASD or the arrow keys move the character and space jumps.
Room 2: switch on Lobby. The investor presses Join and drives the same character with the pad or by clicking. Start zombie wave. Then Next room.
Room 3: they press the waitlist button or walk to a pod, and the launch plays.
